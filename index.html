09 / FAQ

Questions founders

What does a performance marketing specialist do?

A performance marketing specialist plans, runs and measures paid campaigns — Google Ads, Meta Ads and other paid media — against business outcomes like qualified leads and cost per lead, instead of impressions alone. The work covers research, strategy, launch, measurement and continuous optimisation.

Which platforms and services do you cover?

Google Ads (Search, Display and Performance Max), Meta Ads, lead generation, WhatsApp marketing, analytics and tracking with GA4 and Google Tag Manager, SEO and organic growth, social media and email marketing.

How are campaign results reported?

Every campaign is tracked with GA4 and Google Tag Manager conversion measurement. Reporting focuses on CPC, CTR, CPL, conversions and lead volume, so it is always clear what each rupee of spend produced.

How do we start working together?

Reach out on WhatsApp, through the contact form above, or on LinkedIn. Every engagement starts with research — your market, audience and competition — before a strategy and campaign structure are built around your objective.

Do you only work with businesses in Hyderabad?

I’m based in Hyderabad and work with clients across India. Campaigns are managed remotely with tracking and reporting online, so distance never slows the work — most client communication happens on calls and WhatsApp.
